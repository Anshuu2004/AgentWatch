# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct & Professional Standards

> **Community Scope:** Shinraya-Tech-Labs Organization, AgentWatch Repositories, Official Discord Community ([https://discord.gg/UT9uaeY46e](https://discord.gg/UT9uaeY46e)), and Mentorship Programs (SSOC / NSOC 2026 / ELUSoC 2026).  
> **Maintainer Authority:** Project Admin & Core Maintainer Council (`@SHAURYASANYAL3`).  

---

## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the AgentWatch community an educational, productive, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of software experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We hold our engineering community to the highest ethical and professional standards, valuing rigor, intellectual honesty, constructive technical debate, and collaborative problem-solving.

---

## 2. Professional Engineering & Collaboration Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a thriving, high-assurance engineering environment:

* **Empathy & Rigor in Code Reviews**: Giving feedback that targets the architecture and code structure, never the individual. Receiving technical critique gracefully.
* **Intellectual Honesty & AI Transparency**: Openly acknowledging the limitations of your implementation, edge-case risks, and technical debt. Submitting unverified or hallucinated LLM-generated code without full comprehension is strictly prohibited.
* **Respectful Technical Disagreement**: Grounding architectural debates in evidence, formal logic, benchmarks, and reproducible tests.
* **Fair Collaboration & Issue Integrity**:
  - Respecting issue assignments: Never opening pull requests on issues assigned to others.
  - Abiding by the assignment cap: Maintaining at most **3 active/concurrent issue assignments**.
  - Honoring the **72-hour progress SLA**: Providing active updates or draft PRs so issues are not blocked or camped.
  - Providing Discord verification: Showing proof of Discord membership when requesting assignments.
* **Community Support**: Helping new contributors get onboarded, answering questions in Discord, and sharing testing insights.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* **Harassment & Derogatory Conduct**: Personal attacks, insulting language, trolling, or public/private harassment.
* **Contest Sabotage & Unethical Gaming**: Plagiarizing code from other contributors, creating sockpuppet accounts to farm issue assignments, or manipulating program ranking leaderboards.
* **Issue Camping & Unresponsive Reservation**: Requesting multiple issues with bare `/claim` comments and holding them idle past the 72-hour deadline, blocking others from contributing.
* **Doxxing & Privacy Violations**: Publishing personal contact details, private Discord messages, or confidential logs without explicit permission.
* **Disruptive Spam**: Mass-tagging maintainers, opening repetitive low-effort PRs, or flooding review threads with unconstructive complaints.

---

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities & Maintainer Authority

Project Maintainers have the authority and duty to clarify and enforce these standards. Maintainers possess final discretion to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and Discord interactions that violate this Code of Conduct or the [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md).

---

## 4. Four-Tiered Enforcement Framework

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be met with fair, proportional, and swift enforcement:

### Tier 1: Formal Warning & Guidance
- **Trigger**: Minor infraction, unconstructive review remarks, bare `/claim` spamming, or minor template omission.
- **Action**: A private or public written warning from a maintainer clarifying the violation and expected behavioral change.

### Tier 2: Assignment Revocation & Cooling-off
- **Trigger**: Repeated low-effort issue camping, idle violations exceeding the 72-hour rule, refusal to abide by `CONTRIBUTING.md`, or disrespectful tone in issue discussions.
- **Action**: Immediate revocation of active issue assignments, closure of affected PRs, and a temporary 7-day restriction from requesting new issues.

### Tier 3: Program Removal & Disqualification
- **Trigger**: Deliberate plagiarism, automated AI-slop spamming, toxic harassment, or contest/leaderboard manipulation.
- **Action**: Permanent disqualification from the active open-source program (SSOC, NSOC, ELUSoC). Maintainers will notify program coordinators with audit evidence.

### Tier 4: Permanent Organization Ban
- **Trigger**: Severe harassment, malicious code injection, security baseline tampering, or doxxing.
- **Action**: Permanent block across all Shinraya-Tech-Labs repositories, organization spaces, and Discord servers.
